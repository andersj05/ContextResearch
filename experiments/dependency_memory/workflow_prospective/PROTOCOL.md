October 1, 2026. Offline design frozen before the new finite audit and runner
implementation. The old model outcomes and development episode E0001 were
already inspected. This is not statistical preregistration of those data.
There is no provider client or live launch in this version.

## Immediate questions

1. Does the existing exact-table checker with fallback preserve any receipt
   state unavailable to the direct projection in the known receipt family?
2. Does the primary final query in an actual task require information from
   before checkpoint B, and are the memory caps binding for direct receipts?
3. Can the independent receipt specification, frozen grader and staged runner
   agree without returning evaluator truth to an actor?

The first question concerns semantic information, not equality of stochastic
model answers under different JSON presentations. Canonicalization is explicit
in the new implementation. No generic checker, compaction or algorithm novelty
is proposed.

Use the twelve combinations of two source snapshots, three check names and
success/failure as a constructed event alphabet. Enumerate every history of
length zero through three. For each, examine three final snapshots (including
an unseen one), seven nonempty check sets and four artifact inventories.
Compare a separately implemented reverse-scan specification with the original
unchanged grader and direct projection. These are 1,885 constructed histories
and 158,340 query comparisons, not independent tasks.

On every history of length at most two, check six cap values and six writer
proposals: original, reversed, padded, omitted, wrong-state and duplicate.
On histories of length at most three, compare two-boundary checked/direct
state under four cap pairs and all seven announced scopes. Child inputs are
bounded parent plus new observations only. Count every disagreement, retain a
counterexample if found, and do not infer live accuracy from semantic equality.
The proposed cap pairs include an empty parent and an empty child.

This is a local audit by the current implementer using independent formulations.
External human grader review remains pending.

## Four-arm runtime and common final check

Primary future model arms are direct receipts, indexed raw recovery, prose and
full history. The old checked writer is an offline diagnostic for redundancy,
not a new paid arm. Two prose writers plus four final proposals give six planned
actor completions per episode, or 72 for twelve prospective episodes. These
counts are proposed only; authorized provider dispatches are zero.

Parent and child memory caps remain 4,096 and 1,536 decoded UTF-8 bytes. All
writers receive the same public receipt rule. Parent sees only initial public
observations; child sees only its admitted parent, new observations and scope;
the final actor sees admitted memory or its declared archive view plus final
public task. Required checks must be a subset of the announced scope.

Direct retains the latest receipt per (snapshot, check), then filters only by
announced scope. Oversized state becomes infeasible empty memory, without
truncation or undeclared recovery. Prose uses the same byte admission rule.
Indexed recovery stores every raw observation and retrieves requested check
records in original order, including failures and old snapshots. Full history
uses the whole public observation stream. These two arms explicitly have
extra history access; archive/index bytes and operations are charged.

Apply the same pure retained-evidence consistency check after every final
proposal. Keep raw exactness, false-ready answers, contradictions, uncheckable
representations, correct answers withheld and deterministic readouts separate.
A veto is not a corrected execution; a consistent answer is not authenticated
or complete. There is no repair call and no grader feedback to actors.

Logical public requests may use at most 262,144 serialized bytes. The previous
100,000-byte transport envelope cannot be presumed to fit two full observed
checkpoints. This cap is distinct from retained memory, tokens and actual wire
size. Do not clip logs. A resource or transport stop preserves its scheduled
identity and leaves all later identities undispatched, including paired arms.

## Evidence, cost and launch gates

The included runner supports controlled fixtures or saved-response replay only.
Its interfaces are not physical sandboxes. Every provider call would require a
fresh audited context, no repository/filesystem/Git/evaluator access, no opaque
state or previous history, and an explicitly admitted archive arm. Sampling seed,
model revision, provider, transport, token limits, prices and allocation remain
unfilled. Schedule seed is 2026100101, not a model sampling seed.

Freeze actual prospective inputs and all request identities before a separate
live launch. No dispatched identity is retried. Missing pairs leave a complete
paired criterion unassessable. Count all writers, guards, archive construction,
storage, retrieval, local CPU/elapsed time, serialized bytes, failed calls and
human setup. Cache-discounted and cache-neutral model costs are separate. Unknown
prices remain unknown and prohibit a production total-cost claim.

Before live use: finish prospective intake and its timing screen; obtain external
grader review; audit the actual provider payload and isolation; pin revision,
sampling/effort parameters, billing, reservations and a new bounded allocation.
Existing interrupted allocations stay closed. The offline method freeze is not
permission to dispatch or evidence that those launch gates have passed.
