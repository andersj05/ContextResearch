## Frozen design

- Seed `2026100987`, eight revision-ledger level-2 tasks, K/F controls only.
- Luna medium through the existing subscription, at most four workers.
- Three stages and six actions; every action must submit a best candidate.
- Sixteen episodes, at most 136 generations including writers and unknowns.
- Require known outcomes, K in [25%,85%], F at least 25%, a K public mismatch
  and no capacity failures. The inherited fresh-control ceiling allowance is true.
- If selected, validate the whole cell on sixteen new tasks with another seed:
  K must solve 7–12, F at least 7, at least four K episodes must have a public
  mismatch, and both arms must be complete and free of capacity failures.
- Only successful independent validation permits a new, fixed 260-pair R/K
  allocation. With six actions its complete generation bound is 5,720.
