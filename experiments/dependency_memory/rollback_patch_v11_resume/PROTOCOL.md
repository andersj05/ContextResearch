# V11 unsent-work recovery amendment — October 13, 2026

This is an operational amendment to the [frozen v11 protocol](../rollback_patch_v11/PROTOCOL.md),
not a new task cohort. It is decided from admission counts and transport
diagnostics before inspection of any live four-arm scores or contrasts.

The original sealed phase stopped after 289 admissions: 284 completed, five
unknown. Its global stop came from an internal error (-32603) in the client's
`account/rateLimits/read` RPC, before generation for that admitted identity.
A separate native usage check showed ordinary included usage available, with
21% of the weekly allowance used. This is not evidence of exhausted usage.
The unknown identity remains unknown regardless of where its error occurred.

## Fixed scientific contract

Keep the exact original 256 tasks, task order, arm order, generation identities,
model and reasoning effort, full task facts, checkpoint files, feedback, failed
patches, shared categorical lessons, three-attempt limits and stopping rules.
Keep the 3,584-admission ceiling cumulatively across both phases, including
every original unknown. Keep 32 model workers and four grading containers.
There is no sample extension, replacement task, repeated identity, unknown-call
retry, paid fallback or classifier change. Each fresh dispatch still performs
the original included-usage check; a new systemic failure still stops admission.

The prior phase must be sealed and independently replayed before recovery.
The amendment binds its manifest, evidence inventory, ledger and audit by hash,
and freezes the new operational source before any fresh model admission.
The old source, bundle, results, receipts and unknowns remain immutable.

## Recovery rule

Import all original calls, events and thread identities into a new cumulative
ledger. Copy all original requests, responses and diagnostics byte for byte.
Retain completed job results and every job containing an unknown admission.
An unknown prefix keeps unknown eligibility; an unknown lesson stays missing,
although its unaffected no-lesson arms may proceed. No received response is
promoted from an unknown call into a completed observation.

Resume only jobs interrupted by the global stop without an unknown admission.
Unstarted prefixes may run, and their dependent arm dispositions are recomputed.
For a partially completed healthy trajectory, reconstruct its already completed
calls from saved receipts, verifying the original identity and exact request.
Only its next never-admitted identity may contact the model. Prior terminal
outcomes are never used to select new tasks, change treatments or extend repair
allowances. The full original task and eligible-failure denominators remain.

## Interpretation and accounting

The original global stop remains permanently visible. The original isolated
content-effect interpretation gate stays **failed** even if the recovery phase
finishes without a further stop. Report descriptive finite-cohort contrasts,
all missing outcomes and their frozen best/worst bounds; do not claim an
uninterrupted confirmatory experiment or erase the earlier calibration departure.
Unknown prefix eligibility remains a separate limitation of these bounds.

Count shared calls once in the cumulative ledger. Report new admissions and
phase wall time separately from cumulative counts and known metered tokens.
Prior phase wall time plus recovery phase wall time excludes intervening
offline preparation. Planning credits are not an observed subscription debit.
An independent audit must establish unchanged imported rows and receipts,
disjoint new identities, cumulative ceiling compliance, exact retained jobs,
replayed requests and executable received outputs, complete disposition
coverage and the permanently failed interpretation gate.

Scripted interrupted-run fixtures are controller tests with zero model calls;
their manufactured failures are excluded from empirical observations.
