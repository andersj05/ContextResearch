# Practitioner source archive policy

The academic corpus is archived as source PDFs in [`../academic/`](../academic/).

Most practitioner sources in this review are canonical web articles rather than
publisher-provided PDFs. They were **not** printed or repackaged as PDFs when
redistribution and rendering rights were unclear. Their URLs, access dates, major
findings, quantitative claims, limitations, and verification records are preserved in
[`../../source-notes/`](../../source-notes/) and indexed in
[`../../catalog/sources.csv`](../../catalog/sources.csv).

An official practitioner PDF may be added here when the publisher provides a lawful,
stable copy. Generated screenshots or print-to-PDF copies should not be treated as an
original source artifact.
