# Instrumented mathematical rollback follow-up

October 8, 2026. Status: all 32 selected tasks are complete; whole-archive
verification and independent regrading pass. Full repository scientific
validation is pending. This is mathematical development evidence from GPT-6
Luna through the user's existing Codex subscription.

Rollback removes the explicit trial record while retaining the same checkpoint,
public feedback and lesson. On 27 eligible paired tasks, R scores 47/54 terminal
draws versus K's 46/54. The task-level R-minus-K mean is **+1.85 percentage
points**, with a descriptive 95% task-resampling interval **[-7.41, +11.11] pp**.
Four tasks favor R, three favor K and twenty tie. These observations establish
neither a reliable quality advantage nor equivalence. They provide a completed,
auditable comparison and a concrete retained-hypothesis case for further design.

## Fixed allocation and task flow

The [protocol](../experiments/dependency_memory/rollback_math_followup_v4/PROTOCOL.md)
freezes 32 new tasks under seed 2026100851: eight integer, eight modular and
sixteen list-coloring instances. Math-only scope was chosen after the
[interrupted v3 study](ROLLBACK_HARDER_DEVELOPMENT_2026-10-08.md) produced recovery
pairs in mathematics while six coding attempts passed initially. This is a
post-observation development choice, not held-out confirmation or a coding claim.
No v3 task or unresolved identity was retried, and the two 16-task blocks are
accounting partitions of one cohort rather than separate experiments.

Task construction, actor prompts, schemas, low initial checkpoint/branch effort,
medium writers and recovery, 4,096-byte writer bound and 131,072-byte full-client
body bound match v3. The transport adds only whitelisted diagnostic reason codes
and event counts; acceptance, no-tool, one-generation/one-usage and unknown-stop
rules are unchanged. All 424 possible identities, including eight excluded
calibration calls, were frozen with source in commit `1e927f8` before outcomes.
Execution finished the fixed allocation without direction-dependent stopping.

Every selected task has a retained disposition: 27 well-formed public mismatches
produce all four arms, three initial candidates pass and stop, one task has a
malformed branch record, and one exhausts both branch slots without a candidate.
The four `no_candidate` flow events span three tasks; two subsequently produce
eligible mismatches. Event counts are not additional tasks. No writer or capacity
loss prevents a scheduled arm. There are no unstarted tasks or unknown terminal
draws. All 27 scheduled K/R pairs complete, passing the six-complete-pair
operational screen; that screen is not a power threshold. The scheduled-pair
missingness bounds equal the observed mean. The five non-recovery tasks do not
have a measured conditional recovery effect.

## Outcomes and useful limits

The conditions are K (retain trial plus lesson), R (remove trial, retain lesson),
Z (remove trial, no lesson) and G (generic replacement summary).

| Condition | Correct / known draws | Correct rate |
|---|---:|---:|
| K | 46/54 | 85.19% |
| R | 47/54 | 87.04% |
| Z | 48/54 | 88.89% |
| G | 44/54 | 81.48% |

Each task has two stochastic draws per arm; draws are repeated measurements,
not independent tasks. Primary uncertainty resamples the 27 paired task
differences 10,000 times with seed 20261009. It is descriptive and conditional
on these constructed tasks, with only three fixed construction strata. No
confirmatory p-value or inference to unseen mathematical families is supplied.

| Construction stratum | Complete pairs | R minus K (pp) |
|---|---:|---:|
| List coloring | 15 | 0.00 |
| Integer constraints | 7 | +14.29 |
| Modular constraints | 5 | -10.00 |

The secondary R-minus-Z mean is -1.85 pp, with descriptive interval
[-12.96, +11.11] pp; R-minus-G is +5.56 pp, with interval [-3.70, +16.67] pp.
All share the same 27-task intersection. Z's largest observed total does not
establish that discarding the lesson helps. Exact repetition of the rejected
candidate occurs once in K and zero times in R, Z and G; semantic repetition
of refuted assumptions remains unreviewed.

In the [coloring-10 case note](../experiments/dependency_memory/results/rollback_math_followup_v4_2026-10-08/coloring10_case_note.json),
the failed branch hypothesizes that both choices for v11 contradict. The retained
lesson and generic summary repeat that hypothesis **explicitly as unverified
uncertainty**. It is not a certified lesson assertion. Independent enumeration
of all 16,384 publicly allowed assignments finds 24 satisfying assignments, all
with v11=2; the check does not use the hidden witness. Removing the explicit
trial therefore does not remove every branch-derived hypothesis from the retained
context. That is an elementary deduction from the saved records. Possible
anchoring of later answers is a hypothesis: two draws do not establish the
mechanism, and no lesson ablation or independent human rating was performed.
