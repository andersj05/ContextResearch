# Evaluation controls for the inspection–recovery pilot

September 19, 2026. Targeted source review and experimental recommendations; no new model runs. Read alongside the [protocol](../docs/EXPERIMENT_PROTOCOL.md), [prior positioning note](LITERATURE_POSITIONING_2026-09-18.md), and [restricted recovery reference](../experiments/dependency_memory/RECOVERY_FRONTIER.md).

## What the source review changes

The next pilot should test whether a model changes its inspection and recovery behavior across the already computed cost regimes. It should also distinguish failure to preserve information from failure to use available information. Sequential ingestion, revision handling, and interactive memory evaluation are established ideas; none should be claimed as our invention.

Two directly relevant papers were read, with versioned URLs and section scope below. This is not an exhaustive novelty search, a code audit, or an independent replication. No published result is transferred to our artifact workflow as an expected effect size.

## Sources actually read

**MemoryAgentBench** ([v1 full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.05257v1); [metadata](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.05257v1); `memoryagentbench2025`). Read Sections 3.1–3.4, 4.1, 4.4/4.4.1, 4.4.3, and A.4. The evaluation ingests chunks sequentially and then asks questions. FactConsolidation orders contradictory updates and instructs agents to prefer later facts. Its retrieval ablations vary chunk size and retrieval count; its latency analysis separates memory construction from query execution. Our interpretation: revisions and incremental ingestion are prior evaluation mechanisms. The reviewed formulation does not supply our two-boundary inspection-versus-recovery comparison. Later arXiv revisions exist but were not compared; this note cites v1 deliberately.

**AMemGym** ([v1 full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.01966v1); [metadata](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.01966v1); `amemgym2026`). Read Sections 3.1–3.4 and 4.1–4.2. The environment grounds simulated conversations in evolving structured states. It compares interactions generated by each evaluated assistant with replayed traces, reports configuration-ranking differences, and distinguishes write, read, and utilization failures. A ground-truth-state condition measures performance when needed information is supplied. Our interpretation: whole interactive runs and information-sufficient controls are relevant precedents. These results do not establish which policy wins in our workflow. The arXiv record reports ICLR 2026 acceptance; this review uses the versioned manuscript, not an independently checked proceedings record.

## Proposed controls for our pilot

These are our design recommendations, derived from the local environment's information contract and informed by the preceding comparisons. They are not descriptions of either source's implementation.

| Control | What to implement or record | What it resolves |
|---|---|---|
| Complete episodes | Let each method act from the same task initialization; pair task instances and public cost schedules. Continue to terminal verification. | Inspection changes later observations and actions; agreement at one checkpoint is not completion. |
| Isolated diagnostic copies | Run recall or dependency probes on copied context and environment states. Discard those branches; never return their questions, answers, feedback, or provider continuation state to a scored episode. | A probe can reveal which fact will matter and can itself create additional memory. |
| Information-sufficient diagnostic | At a separate terminal checkpoint, supply the exact current required receipt through a labeled evaluator intervention, then test submission. Keep this outside the matched-policy cost comparison. | Failure with the needed receipt visible points to execution/utilization problems. Success does not identify the earlier cause by itself. |
| Dependency and revision diagnostics | Record whether the candidate set was inferred correctly, whether a retained receipt is current, whether recovery was requested, and whether the final payload matches. | Wrong inference, stale retention, missed recovery, and bad submission should not become one undifferentiated memory error. |
| Matched recovery | Give compared methods the same public tool availability, response semantics, and prices within each condition. Log every admitted attempt and rejected action. | A method with extra history access is operating under a different information contract. |
| Separate resource ledgers | Preserve synthetic action cost, serialized bytes, record slots, admitted/model tokens, provider billing buckets, latency, and diagnostic overhead as separate fields. | More retrieval or a shorter prompt cannot by itself establish lower total cost. |

In a diagnostic copy, successful recall is evidence of accessible information under that probe. Failed recall does not prove the information was absent from opaque state or a coded representation. For the initial atomic-record policy class, exact retained-record inspection provides a firmer diagnosis than model self-report. A later prose-memory experiment would need a different attribution rule.

## Narrow testable claim

Keep the claim at the behavioral level: under a frozen task distribution, memory interface, revision schedule, and cost table, does the policy inspect more often when the exact restricted reference says inspection is useful, and avoid it when recovery or sufficient capacity makes it wasteful? Report terminal success and all costs even when the policy chooses poorly.

The exact reference uses reliable recovery, atomic-record retention, uniform routes, and nonbinding episode limits. It calibrates that class. It is not an optimality certificate for arbitrary natural-language summaries or a finite-budget model agent. The pilot must preserve this boundary when describing policy regret or distance from the reference.

A small pilot can uncover an invalid interface, uninterpretable diagnostics, or failure to respond to costs. It cannot establish broad superiority, native-provider savings, or a population effect from a handful of reused templates. Treat any such failure as evidence for revising the mechanism or study design, not as permission to drop unfavorable regimes.

## Remaining review boundaries

This note does not validate the sources' general claims about all memory benchmarks, benchmark reliability, or proprietary systems. It does not audit whether their released code isolates evaluation probes. The probe-isolation rule above is our proposed control, not an allegation of leakage in those papers. Before implementing a provider adapter, recheck its official state-continuity and billing contracts as required by the [protocol](../docs/EXPERIMENT_PROTOCOL.md).
