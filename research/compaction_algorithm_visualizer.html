Read the proposed algorithm
checkpoint(history):
  1. Find the latest request and whether it already has a reply.
  2. Resolve superseded facts; keep current settings and constraints.
  3. Describe repeated events once, preserving relevant outcomes.
  4. Carry unresolved obligations and their exact supporting evidence.
  5. Write state, evidence, and the next action as a continuation checkpoint.
This is a deterministic teaching model inspired by the local research analysis. A production implementation would also need a token budget and a policy for what to do when required evidence does not fit. This demo does not simulate those choices or call a model.

What these examples can and cannot establish
They show an interaction pattern, selected recent evidence, and next-response guidance surviving in recovered renderings. In the two longer cases, that guidance conflicts with the actual last assistant turn. We cannot isolate whether the compactor or the recovery decoder introduced the inconsistency. The data do not establish a fixed summary budget, byte-exact recovery, or performance on real engineering tasks. Read the full inspection.

What happens after recovery?
build_sft_v2.py pairs source conversations with recovered targets and a locally authored checkpoint instruction. It exports training views. The package includes no optimizer, training loop, or trained-compactor evaluation. Its targets are explicitly unverified plaintext, not gold labels.
