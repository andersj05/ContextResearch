## Search stopping rule reached

The search stopped when each synthesis claim family had primary evidence, important
counterevidence, and at least one implementation or explicit limitation, and targeted
gap searches returned mainly recombinations of the same loop, graph, actor, memory,
verification, and execution mechanisms. This is **mechanism saturation**, not literal
exhaustiveness over an unbounded and fast-changing web.

The focused self-improvement pass reached the same stopping condition after the theory,
empirical, counterevidence, and implementation waves. Future additions should supply at
least one of: a controlled ablation, independent replication, a genuinely untouched
outer test, public task-level traces and full search costs, a new theorem with weaker
assumptions, a probability-sampled verifier audit, or a materially new editable object or
control topology.
