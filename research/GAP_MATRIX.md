# Evidence Gap Matrix

| Claim family | Evidence needed | Current support | Contradictions / threats | Confidence | Next query |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Harness definition and boundary | Papers plus implementation evidence | Surveys, 18 explicit harness papers, and 18 versioned systems support a state/context/action/execution/update boundary | “Agent,” “scaffold,” “workflow,” product, and evaluator packaging remain inconsistent | High for working definition; medium for universal terminology | Add only a new formal boundary or materially different topology |
| Tool-use loop | Controlled studies and runtime implementations | ReAct, Toolformer, ToolLLM, coding-interface studies, and current runtimes cover learned, prompted, typed, retrieved, shell, and code actions | Model, tool roster, schema, retrieval, budget, and execution feedback often move together | High that interface matters; medium on optimal interface | Controlled cross-model schema/roster ablation with matched capabilities |
| Planning/search | Ablations under fixed model and budget | ToT, RAP, LATS, self-consistency, test-time compute, repeated sampling, and verifier-limit work | Search gains can be compute, oracle feedback, selector leakage, or public-task tuning; finite optima are conditional | High on coverage/selection decomposition; medium on controller rankings | Cost-matched adaptive search with held-out imperfect verifier |
| Reflection/verification | Independent replications and failure analyses | Reflexion, Self-Refine, CRITIC, process verification, cannot-self-correct, and role-relabeling counterevidence | Intrinsic critique may add no information; execution/oracle cues and extra calls confound gains | High that external information matters; medium on task-specific effects | Preregistered feedback-channel factorial study |
| Memory/context | Longitudinal tasks and retrieval ablations | RAG, Generative Agents, MemGPT, MemoryBank, HiAgent, Lost-in-Middle, LongMemEval, STALE, plus practitioner context reports | Retrieval miss, summary loss, position, staleness, cache cost, and model changes are confounded | High on tradeoffs; low-medium on universal policy | Matched-token delayed-dependency study and rate-distortion model |

| Coding harnesses | Repository architecture plus benchmark protocols | SWE-agent, Agentless, OpenHands/SWE papers, an 18-runtime-system code/docs survey, 14 focused optimizer profiles, and benchmark families | Rapid versions, proprietary services, project-authored evaluations, task leakage, and environment drift | High for pinned mechanisms; medium for comparative efficacy | Re-run a small balanced model×harness×budget study on rotating tasks |
| Evaluation validity | Statistical and contamination analyses | SWE-bench audits, SWE-bench+, SWE-rebench, rigorous benchmark guidance, tau-bench consistency, CORE-Bench, HAL, and infrastructure study | Selected-audit bias, verifier false negatives, contamination, task clustering, and hidden infrastructure | High that scalar scores are conditional; medium on corrected prevalence | Probability sample of passes/failures and versioned outer holdout |
| Cost/reliability mathematics | Sequential-decision and reliability models | pass@k/pass^k, beta-binomial extension, verifier PPV, sequential stopping, reliability chains, label correction, mixed effects, bootstrap, Pareto, and horizon models synthesized | Independence, exchangeability, calibration, stationarity, and utility assumptions require testing | High for stated identities; proposed models require validation | Prove bounds/identification and estimate on repeated open-harness traces |

| Practitioner operations | Public traces, denominators, controls, uncertainty, and replication | 16 first-party reports expose durable state, context, tool, eval, infra, and control-plane practices | Vendor incentives, selected cases, private tasks, changing models, and missing denominators | Medium for failure modes; low-medium for effect magnitudes | Replicate one bounded claim rather than expanding anecdote count |
| Automatic harness optimization | Locked nested evaluation, cost accounting, and independent reproduction | 67 focused papers and 14 pinned optimizer/self-evolution implementations span prompt, context, tool, graph, workflow, code, model-assignment, and policy search | Reused validation, search cost, baseline drift, expanding candidate classes, and same-model generation/evaluation remain endemic | High for mechanism taxonomy; provisional for claimed transferable gains | Test a bounded context compiler with separate search, metered gate, sealed test, and future cohort |

## Highest-value unresolved gaps

1. An operational task-relevant rate--distortion frontier for an editable context compiler.
2. Promotion rules that remain valid under adaptive harness search and repeated evaluation.
3. Sealed-test and later-cohort estimates of whether accepted harness edits actually transfer.
4. Verifier sensitivity and specificity from probability samples of accepted **and** rejected outputs.
5. Balanced model-by-harness-by-budget experiments with infrastructure blocks and total search cost.
6. Formally verified replay, cancellation, authority, rollback, and budget invariants for a small editable harness kernel.
7. Correlated-retry estimates and equal-cost multi-agent baselines on public task-level traces.
