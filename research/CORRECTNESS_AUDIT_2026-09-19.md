| Stage-B outcome | Reliable recovery and scripted submission can conceal total forgetting. | The offline empty-retention control succeeds on all 180 regime/route combinations with unchanged memory capacities. Make pre-recovery availability and completion cost the retention outcomes; terminal success checks execution/schema validity. This is a controlled invariant, not 180 LLM trials. |
| Tiny held-out sample | One route per family confounds route and family. Population cost preferences also need not appear in four sampled routes. | Require fixed-policy comparisons on the same reserved routes, retain sample ties, and prohibit family-effect/generalization claims from this design. | Request labels | Evaluator scenario names such as `costly_recovery` and derived IDs can suggest the intended answer. | Explicitly exclude scenario/pair/episode IDs, reference labels/costs, seeds, routes, and verifier state from future outbound requests. Payload-level tests remain an implementation gate; no leakage in a completed model run is alleged. |

The sampled comparison is generated by [pilot_plan.py](../experiments/dependency_memory/pilot_plan.py): a reference policy is compiled from the full public distribution before reserved routes are used for evaluation. On the four reserved held-out routes, both `costly_recovery` and `revision_cheap_recovery` have mean extra cost one with either inspection choice. Their full-distribution preferences differ. ## Mathematical and source review

The finite-result review checked the reduction from at most four parent cells to exactly four, enumeration uniqueness, all child splits modulo label exchange, coordinate-majority decoding, the integer objective, source-independent randomization under hard capacities, and the 192-outcome normalization. The full rerun checked all **171,798,901** partitions, matching the saved maximum gain 42, error 54/192, 128 attaining partitions, and complete score histogram. The [derivation](EXACT_CHAIN_OPTIMUM_2026-09-19.md) still states that its computational lower bound relies on the reduction and correct execution; a histogram alone is not a proof object.

The block-family review found no invalid step in the exact 3k requirement, signature-entropy bound, or information-inequality direction supporting the conservative all-k excess of 1/512. The one-block excess 1/32 does not replace that uniform bound. The [prior proof audit](PROOF_AUDIT_2026-09-18.md) and [scaling derivation](CREATIVE_RESEARCH_DIRECTIONS_2026-09-18.md) remain local mathematical work awaiting external checking and novelty comparison.

The targeted source recheck verified the new bibliography metadata and experimental descriptions against [MemoryAgentBench v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.05257v1) and [AMemGym v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.01966v1). Sequential updates, interactive evaluation, and information-sufficient diagnostics remain prior ideas, not our novelty claims. Our proposed probe-isolation rule is clearly identified as our control rather than an audited property of those implementations. The close theory spot-check confirmed the intermediate encoder and side-information distinctions in [Ahmadi et al., Sections II-A and III-A](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1207.2793v1); its asymptotic result is not automatically our finite hard-capacity result. See the [evaluation review](PILOT_EVALUATION_REVIEW_2026-09-19.md) for exact review scopes. This was not a re-review of the inherited 171-paper archive.

## What to do next, in order

1. **Build the development interface offline.** Implement `direct_artifact_tags`, the allowlist serializer, and a scripted fake client. Validate every route, both memory boundaries, revision ordering, invalid-output behavior, and complete request/cost logs. Holding public input fixed while changing a private target must leave earlier requests unchanged. Deleted receipts and evaluator labels must never reappear through an undeclared field or session.
2. **Require diagnostic controls before adding a provider.** An exact scripted manager should attain the reference. A forget-all manager should recover successfully while paying the predicted cost. Invalid selections must fail visibly. Full-memory and answer-visible controls should distinguish verifier/adapter failure from retention. Check cost reservation with fake prices and request failures; no credentials are needed.
3. **Complete and freeze the pilot launch contract.** Implement and validate the four reserved held-out renderers without examining model responses, then pin prompts, fixtures, client/runtime, model revision, state-continuity settings, official prices, token limits, and a dollar ceiling. Keep the existing request ceiling; do not silently add retries or diagnostics. A first development tranche is at most 12 Stage-A plus 84 Stage-B-development requests, or **96 total**, after its allocation is specified. The remaining held-out schedule is not an automatic commitment to spend.
4. **Use development to decide whether the question is informative.** Inspect retention and cost differences, not recovered success alone. Preserve null/adverse results. The four-route held-out schedule is a debugging check; a population or template-effect claim needs a separately planned crossed family/route design and defensible sample size. In parallel, seek external mathematical review and complete the narrow novelty comparison before presenting a new theorem or practical improvement.

This audit narrows what a future experiment may claim; it does not supply missing LLM evidence, provider guarantees, broad novelty certification, or proof that every repository file is correct.
