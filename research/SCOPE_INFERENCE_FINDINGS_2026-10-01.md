# Source-only scope inference: completed dispatch, incomplete primary comparison

October 1, 2026. **Live inspected-development evidence, with one permanent
transport gap.** Claim C61. One public program, seven observed answers and a
constructed finite catalog do not establish independent-task accuracy, universal
semantic correctness, publication novelty or production savings.

## Frozen design and information access

The [original protocol](../experiments/dependency_memory/scope_inference/PROTOCOL.md)
predeclared four paired replicates of `concise` and `closure` instructions,
eight calls and a 20-token-derived-planning-credit ceiling. The learner received
the pinned original `exact` and `classify_attempt` functions, constants and a
three-array retention DSL. The historical records, handwritten projection,
catalog, counterexamples and evaluator labels were excluded from its requests.
The closure prompt additionally mentioned membership, absence, malformed types
and container nonemptiness. No outcome feedback or prompt adjustment occurred.

Each call used the recorded `gpt-6-luna` alias at medium effort through the
existing ChatGPT-sign-in app-server transport. The declared global instructions
and fixed tool specification were included as background; tools, repository
access, previous sessions, archives and retained reasoning were unavailable to
the learner. The local process/isolation controls were checked with fresh
zero-generation loopback tests. Immutable backend revision, model sampling seed
and actual account debit remain unavailable. Public source and codebooks are
distinct from instance memory; this is not a native compaction test.

The original source/request freeze was `313a877`, following the design commit
`3abb7f7`. Its approved launch stopped locally at `2de4672` because the durable
reservation directory was missing: **zero dispatches, zero metered answers and
zero unknown-usage reservations**. The failed launch bytes remain preserved.
The [separate corrected launch](../experiments/dependency_memory/scope_inference_v2/PROTOCOL.md)
was frozen at `ea5c450` with new local identities and identical public requests.
It dispatched three identities: two metered answers and one 240-second timeout.
The timeout stays permanently missing, with its 7.65 reservation retained.

The [separate suffix](../experiments/dependency_memory/scope_inference_suffix/PROTOCOL.md)
was frozen at `cdc3518` and completed only the five never-dispatched identities,
in the original order with unchanged public request bytes and scoring. The first
two answers were known before its freeze; selection used dispatch disposition,
and no feedback was added. Its starting accounting included all three prior
attempts and 7.712325 committed planning credits. Neither launched version was
resumed and the timed-out identity was never retried.

## Strong controls and cost accounting

The [separately checked public-schema control](SCOPE_CODEBOOK_CONTROL_2026-10-01.md)
uses the existing fixed dictionary on the same authored fields: 1,957 parent
bytes and 949–1,009 child bytes, scoring 24/24 under identical caps. Cached
public-classifier outcomes score 24/24 with 1,539 parent bytes and 761–779 child
bytes. Neither needs a model extraction call. These controls preclude interpreting
the plain-JSON failures as an information-theoretic obstruction or a gain over
the best available baseline. The conservative literal-root AST control is also
catalog-complete but exceeds the parent cap at 23,741 bytes; raw records take
55,031 bytes and exceed it as well.

Known metered cost is **0.2232910 planning credits**; the cache-neutral view is
0.2399950. The permanent timeout retains **7.65 unknown-usage reserved credits**.
Their sum, **7.8732910**, is committed allocation, not measured spending, and is
below the original 20-credit ceiling. All eight allowed dispatches are used.
API-dollar spending is zero; actual subscription debit and a fully priced
lifecycle comparison remain unknown. Local extraction/classification/serialization
operations and their CPU/elapsed observations are recorded separately and unpriced.

The next empirical question should separate dependency inference from the choice
of codec and include a zero-model source-analysis control that narrows subtree
reads. Changing future query programs would also test when cached answers cease
to be an economical sufficient statistic. Either extension needs a separate
program-family/access contract and frozen evaluator; do not change this completed
dispatch schedule or relabel inspected inputs as untouched natural tasks.
