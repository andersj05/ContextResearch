# li-2025-agent-paradigms — A Review of Prominent Paradigms for LLM-Based Agents: Tool Use, Planning Including RAG, and Feedback Learning

## Why this source is in the corpus

Supplies vocabulary and comparison axes for formalizing the harness as a composite decision system.

## Research question

What major paradigms organize tool use, planning and retrieval, and feedback learning in LLM agents?

## Harness mechanism studied

Tool invocation, RAG-assisted planning, and feedback-driven adaptation.

## Method and experimental setup

Structured review of prominent paradigms with comparative discussion of strengths, weaknesses, and benchmarks.

## Main findings

- The review connects tool use, planning or RAG, and feedback learning as coupled rather than independent modules.
- It emphasizes that gains depend on task and evaluator design.

## Mathematical content

Primarily a taxonomy; mathematical formulations summarize cited retrieval, planning, and learning objectives.

## Evidence quality and limitations

Review-level evidence cannot isolate component effects and terminology overlaps across categories.

## Important implementation details

Use the three paradigms as interacting experimental factors, with budgets and information access held fixed.

## Claims this source supports

Harness design is a coupled optimization problem rather than a menu of independent features.

## Claims this source weakens or contradicts

Claims that one universal agent paradigm dominates across tasks.

## Relevance to a mathematics paper

Motivates interaction terms in a factorial model of harness mechanisms.
