# wang-2024-autonomous-agent-survey — A Survey on Large Language Model based Autonomous Agents

## Why this source is in the corpus

Supplies vocabulary and comparison axes for formalizing the harness as a composite decision system.

## Research question

What components, applications, and evaluation practices define LLM-based autonomous agents?

## Harness mechanism studied

Planning, memory, tool use, and action modules arranged around a foundation model.

## Method and experimental setup

Broad literature survey organized by construction, applications, and evaluation.

## Main findings

- The survey documents recurring planning, memory, and tool interfaces across early agents.
- It shows that evaluation was fragmented across task-specific benchmarks and qualitative demonstrations.

## Mathematical content

Mostly taxonomic; MDP and planning notation are inherited from cited work rather than developed into new guarantees.

## Evidence quality and limitations

Rapidly dated and broad; inclusion and comparison do not establish causal effectiveness.

## Important implementation details

Useful as a terminology map and backward-citation source, not as a quantitative effect estimate.

## Claims this source supports

Harness mechanisms recur across nominally different agent products.

## Claims this source weakens or contradicts

Framework popularity or citation frequency is not evidence of engineering superiority.

## Relevance to a mathematics paper

Defines candidate factors for a factorial model of agent performance.
