# fernando-2023-promptbreeder — Promptbreeder: Self-Referential Self-Improvement Via Prompt Evolution

## Why this source is in the corpus

Directly studies reusable harness components, outer-loop optimization, self-modification, or the statistical controls needed to decide whether an update is a real improvement.

## Research question

Can an evolutionary process improve task prompts while simultaneously evolving the mutation prompts that generate variants?

## Harness mechanism studied

Two coupled populations evolve task prompts and mutation operators through tournament selection, mutation, crossover-like operations, and fitness on sampled training batches.

## Method and experimental setup

PromptBreeder initializes task and mutation prompts, repeatedly selects candidates by tournament on random batches, applies zero-order and self-referential mutations, and returns the best task prompt for evaluation.

## Main findings

- Source-reported Table 1 gives PaLM-2-L PromptBreeder scores of 99.7 MultiArith, 96.4 SingleEq, 87.8 AddSub, 90.2 SVAMP, 71.8 StrategyQA, 85.4 CommonsenseQA, 62.2 AQuA, and 83.9 GSM8K; the reported OPRO aggregate comparator is about 80.2.
- Comparator model and split choices vary, several tasks use author-created half splits, and no independent replication or confidence intervals establish that co-evolving mutations caused the gains.

## Mathematical content

Fitness is empirical accuracy f(p;B) on a sampled batch B; tournament selection approximates argmax over a discrete prompt population, without a monotonicity or convergence guarantee.

## Evidence quality and limitations

Mixed or borrowed baseline results weaken direct comparisons; repeated adaptive fitness calls can overfit small training splits; mutation quality and compute are confounded; dispersion across evolutionary runs is incompletely reported.

## Important implementation details

Maintain task-prompt and mutation-prompt populations, sample training batches, rank via tournaments, apply mutation operators generated by the LM, preserve elites, and evaluate the selected prompt on the held-out split.

## Claims this source supports

Harness text and even the proposal operator can be optimized jointly by derivative-free search.

## Claims this source weakens or contradicts

Evolutionary terminology does not by itself demonstrate open-ended self-improvement, and benchmark gains do not isolate the value of self-referential mutations.

## Relevance to a mathematics paper

Instantiates a stochastic population process whose selection pressure, evaluation noise, and adaptive-overfitting rate can be modeled explicitly.
