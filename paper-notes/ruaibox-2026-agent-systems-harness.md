## Research question

How should harness engineering be defined and organized across agent systems?

## Harness mechanism studied

Joint optimization of workflow, memory, skills, context, error recovery, and multi-agent orchestration around a model.

## Method and experimental setup

Systematic survey and comparative taxonomy of agent systems, optimization strategies, and benchmarks.

## Main findings

- The paper formalizes harness engineering as infrastructure that closes the gap between a single-turn generator and stateful problem solving.
- It treats model and harness as synergistic and catalogues design tradeoffs rather than establishing a causal effect size.
