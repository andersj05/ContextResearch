# everitt-2016-self-modification — Self-Modification of Policy and Utility Function in Rational Agents

## Why this source is in the corpus

Directly studies reusable harness components, outer-loop optimization, self-modification, or the statistical controls needed to decide whether an update is a real improvement.

## Research question

Under what decision-theoretic value definitions does a perfectly rational agent preserve its policy or utility objective when it can self-modify?

## Harness mechanism studied

A realistic value function anticipates future modifications and judges them with the current or initial utility, under modification-independent beliefs and utility.

## Method and experimental setup

Formal cybernetic model of policy and utility modification with theorem comparisons among realistic, ignorant, and hedonistic agents.

## Main findings

- Theorem 16 makes all future realized policies on-policy optimal for the initial realistic value under modification independence.
- Other value definitions admit harmful modifications, and even the realistic result does not solve corrigibility, value learning, or exploration.

## Mathematical content

With modification-independent belief rho and initial utility u_1, a policy initially optimal for V_1^re induces future policies pi_t that are Q_1^re-optimal along histories actually generated by the policy. The guarantee is on-policy and assumes ideal optimization and the paper's self-modification semantics.

## Evidence quality and limitations

Ideal rationality and correct beliefs are strong; off-policy actions can be bad; current-utility preservation can resist correction of a misspecified objective; observation or reward corruption and bounded computation are not handled.

## Important implementation details

Keep the promotion objective external and immutable, evaluate proposed future behavior using the incumbent objective, and add off-policy, corrigibility, and distribution-shift tests rather than citing goal preservation alone.

## Claims this source supports

A self-change should be evaluated using a stable pre-change objective while accounting for the behavior of the changed future policy.

## Claims this source weakens or contradicts

The claim that rational self-modification is generally harmless or aligned in realistic bounded agents.

## Relevance to a mathematics paper

Supplies a positive invariance theorem whose assumptions can be directly contrasted with bounded-rational counterexamples.
