# xi-2023-rise-agent-survey — The Rise and Potential of Large Language Model Based Agents: A Survey

## Why this source is in the corpus

Supplies vocabulary and comparison axes for formalizing the harness as a composite decision system.

## Research question

How should LLM agents be conceptualized across brain, perception, and action modules?

## Harness mechanism studied

A brain-perception-action architecture with profiling, memory, planning, and tool-mediated environment interaction.

## Method and experimental setup

Survey synthesizes methods and applications and proposes a general conceptual framework.

## Main findings

- It helped establish the now-common decomposition of agent systems into model-centered reasoning plus external perception and action.
- The paper identifies memory, planning, and feedback as open engineering bottlenecks.

## Mathematical content

Uses qualitative mappings to agent and decision-process concepts; no new estimator or theorem.

## Evidence quality and limitations

Very broad scope, mostly pre-2024 evidence, and limited controlled comparison between mechanisms.

## Important implementation details

Its component map is helpful for naming harness boundaries but must be updated with event sourcing, sandboxing, and evaluation controls.

## Claims this source supports

The surrounding execution system is part of the agent's effective behavior.

## Claims this source weakens or contradicts

Anthropomorphic labels such as brain do not supply an operational specification.

## Relevance to a mathematics paper

Supplies a first-pass factorization whose variables can be operationalized mathematically.
