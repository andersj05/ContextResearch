# gao-2025-self-evolving-agent-survey — A Survey of Self-Evolving Agents: On Path to Artificial Super Intelligence

## Why this source is in the corpus

Directly studies reusable harness components, outer-loop optimization, self-modification, or the statistical controls needed to decide whether an update is a real improvement.

## Research question

What artifacts, timescales, feedback signals, and architectures characterize self-evolving agents?

## Harness mechanism studied

The survey organizes evolution by what changes—model, memory, tools, and workflow—when it changes, and how scalar or textual feedback drives single- or multi-agent adaptation.

## Method and experimental setup

A broad literature review builds a what/when/how taxonomy, catalogs evaluation and applications, and discusses safety, scalability, personalization, and co-evolution rather than testing a new optimizer.

## Main findings

- The reviewed corpus supports a distinction among evolving weights, memories, tools, and workflow/architecture, and among intra-test-time and inter-test-time adaptation.
- The paper explicitly frames the area as rapidly forming with negotiated boundaries; it supplies no pooled effect size or controlled evidence that self-evolving agents progress toward the ASI language used in its framing.

## Mathematical content

The contribution is taxonomic; formal RL, evolutionary, and optimization equations are inherited from cited methods, with no new estimator, theorem, or meta-analytic model of effect heterogeneity.

## Evidence quality and limitations

Selection and coding procedures are not a preregistered systematic review, rapidly dated preprints dominate parts of the corpus, publication bias is unmeasured, and aspirational ASI framing exceeds the empirical evidence.

## Important implementation details

Use the taxonomy as metadata: editable artifact, update timing, feedback type, single/multi-agent topology, persistence, evaluator, benchmark, safety mechanism, and deployment scope.

## Claims this source supports

Self-improvement should be decomposed by artifact, temporal stage, signal, and control architecture rather than treated as a single capability.

## Claims this source weakens or contradicts

Citation volume and taxonomic inclusion do not demonstrate causal effectiveness, autonomous improvement, or safety.

## Relevance to a mathematics paper

Useful for defining categorical variables and a cross-study design matrix; it does not provide a statistical synthesis.
